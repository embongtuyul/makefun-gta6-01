HEAT ON THE SHORE
Six jobs. One crew. A whole city that remembers what you did.
WASD drive / walk  ·  E enter car  ·  Mouse aim, Click shoot  ·  1–5 weapons  ·  Z / X / C crew  ·  Space handbrake  ·  P pause

HOW TO PLAY

Solmara is yours: a sun-soaked coastal city. Walk it or steal any car and drive it. The yellow marker is always your next job — follow it.

Wanted: crime raises stars. Cops chase, ram and roadblock — and they shoot, so keep moving. Break line of sight and lie low to cool off (watch the IN SIGHT pill) — or call Dana (Z) to swap plates.

Crew: Z Swap Plates · X Quick Fix (Bram repairs your car) · C Eyes Out (Juno marks every cop on the map).

Driving: Shift is nitro while driving. Drift with Space + steering, and near-miss traffic at speed — both pay cash.

Missions: six staged jobs from Selma the fixer. Finish them to raise your rank from Rookie to Shot Caller.

Save: progress auto-saves after every job. Continue any time from the start screen.
